# Artificial Night Sky Brightness Across India

## Overview

This project presents a GIS-based visualization of artificial night sky brightness across India using ArcGIS. The map illustrates the spatial distribution of nighttime illumination and highlights areas experiencing higher artificial light intensity, particularly around major urban centres.

Nighttime light data are useful for understanding urbanization, human activities, infrastructure development, and the spatial patterns of artificial illumination. This project demonstrates the application of Geographic Information System (GIS) techniques for visualizing nighttime brightness across the Indian subcontinent.

## Map Visualization

The map displays the spatial distribution of artificial night sky brightness across India, including state boundaries, major cities, geographic coordinates, and nighttime light intensity.

The visualization uses a grayscale raster representation to highlight variations in artificial illumination, with brighter areas indicating regions of greater nighttime brightness.

## Objectives

- To visualize the spatial distribution of artificial night sky brightness across India.
- To identify regions exhibiting relatively high nighttime illumination.
- To examine spatial patterns of artificial lighting in major urban centres.
- To demonstrate GIS-based raster visualization and cartographic techniques.
- To develop a thematic map representing nighttime light intensity across India.

## Methodology

The map was prepared using the following GIS workflow:

1. Imported nighttime light raster data into ArcGIS.
2. Added the administrative boundary shapefile of India.
3. Applied raster symbology to visualize nighttime brightness intensity.
4. Adjusted the grayscale colour representation to distinguish illuminated and less illuminated regions.
5. Added state boundaries and major city locations.
6. Incorporated geographic coordinates, a north arrow, scale bar, legend, and map title.
7. Prepared the final thematic map using ArcGIS Layout View.

## Software and Tools

- **ArcGIS 10.8**
- Raster data visualization
- Shapefile-based administrative boundaries
- GIS cartographic design
- Spatial data processing

## Key Observations

- Major urban centres exhibit comparatively higher nighttime illumination.
- Artificial illumination shows spatial variation across different regions of India.
- Urban and densely populated regions display prominent nighttime brightness patterns.
- The map provides a geographical overview of artificial nighttime lighting distribution.

## Project Output

The final output is a thematic GIS map titled **"Artificial Night Sky Brightness Across India"**, representing the spatial distribution of nighttime light intensity across India.

## Applications

Artificial night sky brightness mapping can contribute to:

- Urbanization and urban growth studies
- Light pollution assessment
- Environmental monitoring
- Sustainable urban planning
- Remote sensing and GIS research
- Assessment of human activities and infrastructure development
